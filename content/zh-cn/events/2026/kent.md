## Overview

The DiagHam-ALPS Developer Workshop brings together developers of the [DiagHam](https://www.nick-ux.org/diagham/) and ALPS projects to coordinate joint development work supported by the DiagHam-ALPS grant.

## Dates

- **December 7-8, 2026:** core workshop days
- **December 9, 2026:** final sessions, ending around lunchtime / early afternoon

## Location

The workshop will be held at the University of Kent in Canterbury, UK. Further details on the venue will be posted here.

## Accommodations

Accommodation will be arranged on campus or nearby for participants. **Please send your planned arrival and departure dates to the organizer** so that rooms can be booked.
